# Ride City 🏍️🛴

A mini Roblox riding game: a **motorbike** (fast) and an **e-scooter** (nimble), a street
grid with two jump ramps, knockable traffic cones, floating coin trails (including air
coins over the jumps), and a **Coins** leaderboard.

Everything (map, vehicles, physics, scoring) is generated by **one script** — no models,
plugins, or assets needed.

## Install (2 minutes)

1. Open **Roblox Studio** → **New** → **Baseplate** template.
2. In the **Explorer**, hover **ServerScriptService** → click **+** → choose **Script**.
3. Delete the default code and paste in everything from `RideCity.server.lua`.
4. Press **Play**. You spawn on the green pad; the red motorbike and green e-scooter
   are right in front of you.

Works in multiplayer too — every player gets the same map and their own coin score.

## Controls (default Roblox vehicle controls)

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| **W / S** (or ↑/↓) | Throttle forward / brake & reverse |
| **A / D** (or ←/→) | Steer (front wheel turns for real) |
| **Space** | Jump off the vehicle |
| **E** near/on the seat | Respawn the vehicle at its start spot |

The motorbike stays upright automatically (arcade-style stabilizer) but can still lean
over ramps and jump freely. If you somehow get it stuck, use the **E – Respawn** prompt
on the seat.

## Gameplay

- Ride around the street grid.
- Collect glowing **coins** (+1 each, shown on the leaderboard).
- Hit the **jump ramps** (north ramp on the vertical road, west ramp on the main road)
  and grab the **air coins** over the landing zone.
- Dodge or smash the orange cones near the northeast intersection.
- Coins respawn 20 seconds after collection.

## Tuning cheat sheet

All knobs live in the `VEHICLES` table at the top of the script:

| Field | Meaning | Moto default | Scooter default |
|---|---|---|---|
| `speed` | Top speed (rad/s at the wheel; higher = faster) | 55 | 32 |
| `torque` | Acceleration strength | 4000 | 2000 |
| `steerAngle` | Max front-wheel angle in degrees | 26 | 34 |
| `color` | Body color | red | green |
| `chassisSize` / `wheelSize` / `wheelGap` | Body and wheel dimensions | — | — |

Map tweaking: change sizes/positions in `buildMap()`, coin spots in `buildCoins()`,
and cone positions in the cones loop.
